# Hey, I'm Adam

Backend engineer obsessed with distributed systems. I specialize in asking "why are we doing this?" and then finding out by pushing technology past its comfort zone.

I design systems that handle scale without falling over at 2 AM. Concurrency, race conditions, caching architectures, sharding strategies, the whole fun mess. The stuff that's easy to get wrong and impossible to ignore.

---

## What I Actually Care About

**Distributed Systems** - Concurrency, consensus, the algorithms that make everyone's head hurt but keep systems alive.

**Performance at Scale** - Database optimization, caching that doesn't just move the problem elsewhere, knowing when to denormalize your schema.

**Architecture That Works** - Systems designed to fail gracefully, scale predictably, and be understood by humans six months later.

**Questioning Stack Choices** - Not every problem needs the trendy solution. Sometimes the boring one is right, sometimes it isn't. Let's find out.

## How I Work

I think about fault domains before I think about code. I know that scaling isn't just throwing more servers at it. I understand that the database is never the bottleneck until it suddenly is.

I've spent enough time in production to have healthy respect for things that can go wrong. Distributed systems taught me that coordinating across machines is harder than it looks and twice as hard as the textbook says.

The best architecture I've written is the one no one has to think about. The worst is the one that made perfect sense in the design doc.
